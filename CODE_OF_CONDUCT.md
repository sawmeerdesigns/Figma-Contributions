# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it.

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's background, identity or experience level.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent, and accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, or publishing others' private information.
- Help newcomers. Questions are welcome.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior concerns you, email **sawmeerdesigns@gmail.com**. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer reviews every report and responds within 7 days, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
